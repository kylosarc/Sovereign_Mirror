## 1. Visual & Physicalization Metaphor

The Sovereign Mirror translates abstract distributed governance dynamics into a luminous, volumetric **Toroidal Mesh**. Rather than cold administrative spreadsheets, the operator observes a living geometric manifold where every node's veracity and consensus health are immediately legible through color, orbital velocity, and geometric resonance.

---

## 2. Particle State Dynamics

| Color Spectrum | Governance Phase | Physical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cyan / Teal Glow** | `01_ACTIVE` | Nominal operation. Nodes emit coherent veracity pulses. |
| **Yellow / Orange** | `02_SYNC` | Convergence phase. P-Gate confirmation cycles increment. |
| **Red / White** | `03_FISSION` | Dumbbell fission stress test. Entropy perturbation applied. |
| **Dim / Gray** | `04_STANDBY` | Rest phase. Ledger commits finalized. |

---

## 3. Decoupled Gate Severities

Aligned with Tropelex procedural governance:
- `T1: GATE_PASS` — Objective veracity confirmed.
- `T2: GATE_WARN_LOG` — Minor noise logged to audit trail.
- `T3: GATE_PROMPT_INTERCEPT` — Subjective noise requiring operator ratification.
- `T4: GATE_PQC_CHALLENGE` — Requires fresh MAYO-1/Kyber signature.
- `T5: GATE_STATE_FREEZE` — Pain floor triggered; consensus frozen.
