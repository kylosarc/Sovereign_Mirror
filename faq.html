What do the particle colors represent in the 3D visual canvas?

Each glowing particle represents an active network node. The color indicates real-time consensus conditions:

  • Cyan / Teal Glow: Active and healthy governance — nodes are submitting valid proofs and reaching consensus.
  • Yellow / Orange: Synchronizing — nodes are coordinating P-Gate confirmation cycles toward quorum.
  • Red / White: High-stress state ("Dumbbell Fission") — network under elevated stress or entropy divergence.
  • Dim / Gray: Standby mode — network in a quiescent rest state.

Why does the Inverion Divide "remediate rather than delete"?

Deleting dissenting or deprecated nodes creates unverified historical gaps and introduces centralized administrative power. Under the Inverion Divide, deprecated nodes and rejected proposals are sequestered into an immutable, auditable state partition. This preserves complete historical provenance while removing malignant vectors from active consensus.
