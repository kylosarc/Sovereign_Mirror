# Architecture Philosophy: Sovereign Mirror & Radical Veracity

## 1. The Right to Absolute Sovereignty
A sovereign is any entity holding ultimate authority over its own cognitive focus and state transitions. Centralized authorities inevitably introduce subjective filtering, telemetry extraction, and administrative vulnerability. The Sovereign Mirror distributes sovereignty across an immutable geometric topology.

## 2. Non-Moral, Graded Demarcation
The Sovereign Mirror makes no claim to universal moral judgment. It implements an **Epistemic Demarcation Box**: transparent, operator-configured token weights that filter structural noise without imposing dogmatic values.

## 3. Remediation Over Erasure
Deletion is the tool of centralized authority; it erases history and obscures failure. The **Inverion Divide** preserves all deprecated or anomalous nodes within an immutable, auditable historical partition.
